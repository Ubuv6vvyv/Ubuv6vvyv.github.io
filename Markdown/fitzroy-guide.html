Island Activities

Walking Tracks

Secret Garden Track

Duration: 45 minutes return

A peaceful walk through tropical rainforest with beautiful views.

Lighthouse Track

Duration: 3.6 km return (2-3 hours)

Steep climb to the lighthouse offering panoramic views of the Coral Sea and surrounding islands.

Summit Track

Duration: 4.2 km return (3-4 hours)

Challenging hike to the island's peak with breathtaking 360-degree views.

Beaches

Welcome Bay

The main beach featuring white coral sand, perfect for swimming and snorkeling.

Nudey Beach

Voted one of Australia's best beaches, a 15-minute walk from the resort. Known for its pristine white coral sand and crystal clear waters.

Snorkeling

Explore the vibrant fringing reef right off the beach! The best snorkeling spots include:

  • Welcome Bay - Perfect for beginners with easy beach entry
  • Nudey Beach - Amazing coral gardens and tropical fish
  • Bird Rock - Advanced snorkelers can spot sea turtles and larger fish

Dining Options
