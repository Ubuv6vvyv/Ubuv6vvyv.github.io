How to Use the Enhanced Collage Generator

  1. Upload Your Images: Click the "Choose Files" button to select the images you want to include in your collage.
  2. Choose a Layout: Select from Grid (automatic), Single Row, Single Column, or Custom Grid layouts.
  3. Customize your Grid: For custom layouts, adjust the rows and columns using the sliders.
  4. Edge Effects:
    • Border Width: Sets the spacing between images
    • Edge Blur: Creates soft transitions between adjacent images
    • Blur Blend Intensity: Controls how strongly the blurred edges blend together
  5. Resolution: Choose the output resolution for your final collage.
  6. Zoom: Use the zoom slider to preview details (this won't affect the final download quality).
  7. Add Numbers: Check the "Add Number Overlay" box to number your images and customize the appearance.
  8. Shuffle & Download: Rearrange your images randomly or download your finished collage.
