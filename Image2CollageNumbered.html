How to Use the Image Collage Generator

  1. Upload Your Images: Click the "Choose Files" button to select the images you want to include in your collage. You can select multiple images at once.
  2. Select the Right Number of Images: Make sure you've selected a number of images that can form a perfect square (1, 4, 9, 16, 25). If you select the wrong number, you'll get an error message.
  3. Generate Your Collage: Click the "Generate Collage" button to create your collage. Your images will be arranged in a grid.
  4. Add a Number Overlay (Optional): If you want to add numbers to each image in your collage, check the "Add Number Overlay" box.
  5. Zoom In or Out: Use the zoom slider to make your collage bigger or smaller.
  6. Download Your Collage: Click the "Download Collage" button to save your collage as a PNG image.
