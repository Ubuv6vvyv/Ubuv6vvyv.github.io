STATEMENT OF CLAIM
BEING RUDE, PROVOCATIVE AND UNKIND
The defendant, on Tuesday, March 18 2025, did knowingly and with malice aforethought, engage in conduct of exceptional rudeness, provocative comments, and unkind behavior towards the plaintiff. Said conduct occurred via WhatsApp chat messages and in person on occasion. The defendant's actions caused significant emotional distress.
