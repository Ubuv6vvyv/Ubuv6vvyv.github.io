Fluid Time Clock

This timepiece uses bold colours and fluid motion to display the current time:

  • Hours (yellow): Glowing markers on a 40% radius ring.
  • Minutes (silver): Tiny markers on a 18% radius ring.
  • Seconds (red): A pulsing red second-hand with a subtle trail.

The layout is spaced out to ensure clear, contrasting visibility.
