HOW TO READ THIS CLOCK

This luxury timepiece displays time using three concentric rotating rings:

  • Inner ring (rose gold): Hours - marked at 3-hour intervals with elegant numerals
  • Middle ring (platinum): Minutes - each mark represents 5 minutes with subtle texture
  • Outer ring (silver): Seconds - each mark represents 1 second with fine detailing

The stationary gold indicator at the top points to the current time across all three rings.

The guilloche center pattern and decorative accent rings create depth and luxury reminiscent of haute horlogerie.
