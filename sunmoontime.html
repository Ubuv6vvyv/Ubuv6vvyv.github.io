How to use:

1. The Compass Dial (with N, E, S, W) rotates to show True North at its 'N' marker.

2. The Orange Sun Line & Icon show the Sun's actual direction on this North-oriented dial.

3. The Blue Line always points to the top of your phone screen.

4. To Find the Sun: Rotate your phone until the orange Sun Line is perfectly aligned under the fixed blue Device Line.
